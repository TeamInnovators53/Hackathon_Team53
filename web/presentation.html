Slide 1 • The Business Challenge

The $25 Monthly Fee Trap & Friction for VIP Clients

Key Privilege Checking accounts represent KeyBank's most profitable relationship clients. However, maintaining the account fee-free requires meeting strict monthly criteria.

Unexpected Service Charges
Clients who miss the cutoff by just a few hundred dollars or delayed payroll receive surprise $25.00 maintenance fees, causing high call volume and fee reversal disputes.
Generic IVR Disconnect
Traditional contact centers subject high-net-worth callers to generic menus, failing to acknowledge their Household Relationship Group tier or proactive fee status.
Repetitive Transfers
When escalated, callers must repeat their account details and problem description because telephony routing lacks warm session context.

Rule 3 (Key@Work Direct Deposits): Be a Key@Work member with direct deposits totaling ≥ $2,500.00 during each cycle.
